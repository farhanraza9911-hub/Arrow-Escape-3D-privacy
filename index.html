This Privacy Policy describes how Farhan Bashir ("we", "us", "our"), the developer of the Android application Arrow Escape 3D (package name com.farhan.arrowmazepuzzle.game, the "App"), collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the App. This policy applies only to the App and governs data handling related to it.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Escape 3D is an offline puzzle game. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, or other personal identifiers through gameplay.

The App stores the following information locally on your device only (never sent to us):

  • Your level progress and unlocked levels
  • Your sound, vibration, and music volume preferences

This local data can be cleared at any time by clearing the App's storage/cache from your device's Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App displays ads using Google AdMob and uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage ad-related consent (for example, the EU/UK consent form shown to eligible users). To provide and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, and general usage statistics)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy, not by us directly. You can review how Google handles this data here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google's EU User Consent Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use AdMob partner apps

Where required (for example, for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the App shows a consent form via the Google UMP SDK before requesting personalized ads. You can change your ad consent choices at any time by reopening that consent form from the App's Settings screen, or by resetting your advertising ID / limiting ad tracking in your device's system settings.

3. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape 3D is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address above so we can address it. (If your Play Console "Target audience and content" declaration includes children, update this section and your ad configuration to use Google's child-directed treatment settings accordingly.)

4. Data Security

Since the App does not transmit personal data to our own servers, we do not operate any backend storing your information. Data collected by third-party ad services is secured and processed under their own security practices, linked above.

5. Your Choices

  • Local data: clear it any time via Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Escape 3D → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the App.

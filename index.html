This Privacy Policy describes how Farhan Bashir ("we", "us", "our"), the developer of the Android application Arrow Escape 3D (package name com.farhan.arrowmazepuzzle.game, the "App"), collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the App. This policy applies only to the App and governs data handling related to it.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Escape 3D is a puzzle game that can be played offline. An internet connection is used to show ads and, only if you choose to create a profile in the Platinum game, to show the Platinum leaderboard (see section 3). We do not require you to create an account, the App has no in-app purchases, and we do not ask for your email address, phone number, or real name.

The App stores the following information locally on your device only (never sent to us):

  • Your level progress and unlocked levels
  • Your sound, vibration, and music volume preferences

If you create an optional Platinum profile, the information listed in section 3 is also sent to our leaderboard database.

This local data can be cleared at any time by clearing the App's storage/cache from your device's Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App displays ads using Google AdMob and uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage ad-related consent (for example, the EU/UK consent form shown to eligible users). To provide and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, and general usage statistics)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy, not by us directly. You can review how Google handles this data here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google's EU User Consent Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use AdMob partner apps

Where required (for example, for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the App shows a consent form via the Google UMP SDK before requesting personalized ads. Where Google's consent rules apply to you, a "Privacy Settings" button is shown in the App's Settings screen so you can change your ad consent choices at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's system settings.

3. Platinum Leaderboard and Optional Profile

In the Platinum game you can optionally create a profile by choosing a nickname and an icon. The profile is optional: if you skip it, nothing below is collected and you can still play every game.

If you create a profile, the App sends the following to our leaderboard database:

  • The nickname you typed and the icon you chose
  • Your highest cleared Platinum level and the time you reached it
  • A random anonymous player ID generated on your device (it is not your Android Advertising ID, device ID, or account)

This information is visible to other players on the leaderboard, so please do not use your real full name or any personal information as a nickname. Nicknames must be appropriate: offensive names are not allowed, are filtered in the App, and can be reported with the flag button on the leaderboard. We may remove nicknames or players that break these rules.

The leaderboard database is hosted by Google Firebase Realtime Database, which processes this data on our behalf over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We use this data only to show the leaderboard and rank players. We do not sell it and do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your profile: open Settings → My Profile → Remove me from the leaderboard in the Platinum game to delete your nickname, icon, and score from our database. You can also email us at farhanraza9911@gmail.com and we will delete it. Uninstalling the App does not by itself delete leaderboard data, so please use the remove button first if you want it deleted.

4. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape 3D is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Platinum profile only asks for a nickname and an icon, never real names, emails, or contact details. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address above so we can delete it.

5. Data Security and Retention

Leaderboard data is sent to Firebase over HTTPS. We do not operate any other backend that stores your information. We keep leaderboard data until you remove your profile or ask us to delete it. Data collected by third-party ad services is secured and processed under their own security practices, linked above.

6. Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: manage this via the in-app "Privacy Settings" button (where shown) or your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Leaderboard profile: create, edit, or remove it any time in the Platinum game (Settings → My Profile).
  • Local data: clear it any time via Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Escape 3D → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Questions or requests: email us at farhanraza9911@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as possible.
